02 — Design directionCareer Ops · Forces edition

Squared away, not camouflaged.

Two of our existing systems, blended. Structure comes from Sidestand: square panels, hairline rules, a 5px accent bar and a hard offset shadow. Palette comes from Thereabouts: olive, limestone and espresso — outdoor and calm, never camo. The transition to civilian life shows up in the copy, not the chrome.

Keep

Every feature, flow and data field of today's Career Ops. This is a reskin, not a rebuild.

Add

Service record, clearance, exit-date countdown and a military-to-civvy skills translator.

Avoid

Stencils, camo, insignia, flags. The audience is leaving uniform — the product shouldn't wear one.

04 — Analysis resultCareer Ops · Forces edition

The verdict leads. The number follows.

GO / proceed with care / NO-GO is the headline, with the AI's recommendation underneath. The reasoning now includes Where your service maps and Clearance & eligibility, so leavers can see how their experience reads to a civilian employer.

06 — Live ScansCareer Ops · Forces edition

Recce the market.

Live search across Reed and Adzuna, as today. New: results that need security clearance are tagged against what the user holds (e.g. SC required · you hold SC), and the employer exclusion defaults to the MOD / their service.

07 — Config: CV & service recordCareer Ops · Forces edition

Service record in.

New section: service, trade, rank, service number (private — never sent to the AI or employers) and clearance held. The exit-date panel counts down to the last day in service and shows progress through notice.

08 — Config: translator, profile, evidenceCareer Ops · Forces edition

Civvy CV out.

The skills translator turns service language into civilian language, and the user decides line by line what goes into their CV. The profile and evidence anchors work as today, with examples written around postings and units.

Your calls.

Name

02
Tone

Is the banter pitched right — "Recce the market", "Kit list", "no gucci titles", "a £5 brew"? More, less, or about right?

04
Skills translator

The AI suggests each translation and the user approves it line by line. Is that the right amount of control?

05
