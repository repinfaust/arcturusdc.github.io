# Career Ops — Getting Started

A tool that reads your CV, scores job ads against your actual experience, and drafts tailored CVs and cover letters. Nothing it writes is invented — everything is grounded in what's in your CV.

Your workspace is **stef - career**. It's yours alone. Nobody else can see what you upload, and you can't see anyone else's.

## 2. Upload your CV (do this first)

Open the **Setup** tab.

Upload your CV, or paste the text in. Then hit the extract option — it reads the CV and fills in two things for you:

- **Your profile** — name, current role, location, the kinds of roles you're targeting, minimum salary
- **Your evidence library** — your real achievements, per role

Check both and fix anything it got wrong. This is the foundation for everything else, so it's worth five minutes. If it's thin or wrong, every score and every draft after it will be too.

Save when you're happy.

---

## 3. Find or add a role

Two ways in:

**Search** — enter keywords, a location, and a minimum salary if you want one. It searches Reed and Adzuna, strips out the obvious noise, then ranks what's left against your target roles.

**Paste a job you've already found** — drop in the full job description text, or the URL of the ad.

> On URLs: it works on most job boards, but some sites block automated reading or sit behind a login. If it can't read the link it'll tell you — just copy the job description text and paste that instead. Pasting the full text always gives the better result anyway.

---

## 4. Analyse it

Hit analyse. You get back:

- A **score out of 5** for how well the role fits you
- A breakdown of where you're strong, where you're light, and what's worth addressing

The role is saved to your pipeline, so it's still there when you come back.

---

## 5. Tailor a CV and cover letter

On a role you like:

- **Tailor CV** — a version of your CV rewritten to emphasise what this employer is asking for, plus a short note on what was changed and why
- **Cover letter** — 250–350 words, tailored to the role. You can ask for revisions in plain English ("make it warmer", "cut the second paragraph")
- **Application answers** — drafts for the usual free-text questions ("why this role", "why us", "what makes you a good fit")

Everything is saved against the role. Tailored CVs also collect in the **CVs** tab.

Important: it re-emphasises what's already in your evidence library. It will not invent a job, a metric, or a skill you don't have. If a draft looks thin for a role, that's usually a sign the evidence library needs more in it — go back to Setup and add detail.

## Suggested first run

1. Sign in
2. Upload your CV, extract, correct anything wrong, save
3. Paste in one real job you'd genuinely apply for
4. Analyse it — does the score match your own gut feel?

That's about five actions and it exercises the whole loop.
