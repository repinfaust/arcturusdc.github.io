# Перевірмо разом · Card artwork

Start with **snow, chess, cat, museum, poster and seed**. Together they cover the first playable exercise. Each image represents the fictional post itself. Dates, timetables, prices and other clues remain in the source panel.

## Shared style prompt

Paste this before the scene prompt for each image:

> Landscape artwork for a Ukrainian-language media literacy game for ages 8–11. An ordinary, inviting contemporary neighbourhood. One clear subject, lively composition, natural proportions, clean shapes and gently textured colour. Where the scene calls for a photograph, use candid natural light and an everyday phone-camera composition. Where it calls for an illustration, use a modern illustrated chapter-book style with soft gouache, expressive silhouettes and small pops of lilac, sky blue, apricot and marigold. Keep important details together in the middle of the frame so a wide crop works on a phone. Render the scene only. No captions, lettering, dates, clocks, watermarks, logos, borders, interface or answer symbols. Use fictional places and objects.

Export in WebP, approximately 1200 × 640, with the filenames below. Keep the main subject inside the middle two-thirds of the image. Supply the images as individual files so they can be reviewed at card size.

## First exercise

| File | Scene prompt |
|---|---|
| `snow.webp` | Candid phone photograph of a small neighbourhood playground covered in fresh snow. A blue slide, two empty swings and a low bench sit in the same group at the centre. Muted winter morning light, pale apartment blocks softly out of focus, no people. The photograph should give no clue what day or year it was taken. |
| `chess.webp` | Gouache illustration of a chess club table. A half-played board, a few captured pieces and warm sunlight from a nearby window. Frame the chessboard from just above table height. Leave out flyers, calendars and time cues. |
| `cat.webp` | Candid phone photograph of a curious tabby sitting beside one visibly empty ceramic bowl on a kitchen floor. Put the cat and bowl close together in the middle of the frame. The cat looks towards the camera. No food, crumbs, licking, second animal or other hint about who emptied the bowl. |
| `museum.webp` | Gouache illustration looking into a little fictional museum of paper cities. Put an intricate paper street with pastel houses, tiny trees and a tram at the centre on a display plinth. No signage, ticket desk, prices or visitors. |
| `poster.webp` | A poster-style gouache illustration of a playful orange cat curled around a pencil on a lilac background. Make the hand-drawn style clear through broad brush shapes and paper grain. Fill the frame with the artwork. No headline, dates or event information. |
| `seed.webp` | Gouache close-up of a terracotta pot filled with dark soil on a sunny windowsill, with a small blue watering can nearby. The soil surface is bare. Show no visible seed, sprout, seed packet or clue to the plant variety. |

## Integration

Review each image beside its post and clue. Keep the same image when an exercise refers to an earlier copy of that post. Check that artwork conveys no extra evidence and that a short Ukrainian alt description states what the picture shows. Record where the image came from and its reuse terms. Place approved assets in `public/img/assumezero/ukraine/`, then add the matching `imageAlt` to each card in `public/games/truthnet/cards-uk-UA.json`. The game shows a topic icon until the artwork is registered.
