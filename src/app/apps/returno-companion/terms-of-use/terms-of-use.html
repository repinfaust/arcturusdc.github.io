1. What Returno provides

Returno is a personal wellbeing record. It helps you describe what you want to return to, record check-ins and activities, note milestones and reflect on your own entries.

Returno is not medical care. It does not diagnose, prescribe treatment, assess whether an activity is safe, or predict recovery. It is not a medical device or an emergency service. Follow advice from qualified health professionals and seek appropriate help for symptoms or concerns. For an emergency, contact your local emergency services.

Charts and comparisons describe your entries. Missing data, self-reported information and ordinary variation limit what they can tell you. They do not establish causes or replace professional assessment.
