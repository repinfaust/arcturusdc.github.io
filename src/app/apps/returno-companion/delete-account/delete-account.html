Account & data deletion

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Returno Companion, provided by Arcturus Digital Consulting, offers two deletion options. You can use them even after your trial expires. You can also request deletion by email without installing or signing in to the app.

Deleting records and deleting an account are different. Choose the option that matches what you want removed. Deletion is permanent; signing out or uninstalling alone does not delete your cloud data.

Option 1: delete records, keep your account

  1. Open Returno Companion and sign in to the account you want to use.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete my records.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm the deletion. Keep an internet connection until the app confirms completion.

This removes your goals, check-ins, activities and milestones from your cloud account and the current device. You keep your sign-in account, app preferences and consent choices. This does not remove your purchase entitlement or its transaction records.

Option 2: delete your Returno account

  1. Open Returno Companion and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete my account. An account-deletion action is also available on the purchase screen.
  3. Read the confirmation and confirm. Keep an internet connection until completion; if deletion fails, follow the message or contact us.

This removes the Returno cloud records associated with your account, including your goals, check-ins, activities, milestones and account settings, and deletes the Firebase Authentication account used by Returno. The app then clears the current device's account data and saved consent choices.

This deletes your Returno account, not your Google account. Check any other devices where you used Returno and remove their local app data too, especially devices that were offline during deletion.

Request deletion without the app

Email info@returno.health with the subject Returno Companion account or data deletion.

  • Send from the Google email address linked to your Returno account where possible. If you cannot access it, tell us so we can discuss another way to verify ownership.
  • Say whether you want records only deleted or your entire Returno account deleted.
  • Tell us if you also want us to review deletion of associated RevenueCat purchase-service information.

Do not send passwords, payment card details or copies of health records. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership before deleting anything. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable data protection law and explain any information that must be retained.

Information handled separately

  • Purchases and trial records: in-app account deletion does not automatically delete RevenueCat customer, alias or purchase records. Request a review through the email address above. Some information may need to be retained for transaction, accounting, legal or fraud-prevention purposes.
  • App-store transactions: Apple and Google control their own transaction records and retention requirements. Deletion does not refund a purchase. Contact the store about refunds or its own account records.
  • Provider logs and previously sent requests: deleting app records does not recall earlier optional AI requests or analytics events. Provider retention rules still apply. For example, OpenAI's standard abuse-monitoring retention can be up to 30 days, with legal or safety exceptions.
  • Copies outside the service: we cannot erase screenshots, device backups or other copies you or another person have kept outside Returno.

In-app deletion removes the active Returno records as part of the deletion operation; it is not a promise that every provider's security, operational or transaction record is erased at the same instant. If a support request involves retained information, we will explain its purpose and the applicable retention period or criteria.

For more detail, read our privacy policy. For help with deletion or restoring a purchase before account deletion, email info@returno.health.
